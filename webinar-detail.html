Advanced EIS Techniques for Battery Research

Master electrochemical impedance spectroscopy for Li-ion and next-gen battery characterisation. Live Q&A with Dr. Elena Torres.

About This Webinar

Electrochemical Impedance Spectroscopy (EIS) is one of the most powerful tools for understanding battery performance, degradation, and internal resistance. In this live session, Dr. Elena Torres will demonstrate advanced EIS techniques specifically for Li-ion and next-generation battery systems.

You'll learn how to design experiments, select appropriate frequency ranges, fit equivalent circuit models, and interpret impedance spectra to extract meaningful insights about your cells.

Whether you're new to EIS or looking to refine your existing workflows, this session will provide practical knowledge you can apply immediately in your lab.
