Corrosion Measurement: Best Practices & Pitfalls

Learn how to get reliable corrosion data from polarisation and Tafel experiments, avoid the most common measurement errors, and interpret your results with confidence. Recorded live with Dr. Mark Stevenson.

Speaker

About This Webinar

Electrochemical corrosion measurements look simple, but small mistakes in cell setup, scan rate, or data fitting can change corrosion rates by an order of magnitude. In this session, Dr. Mark Stevenson walks through the techniques used to measure corrosion in the lab and shows where results most often go wrong.

You'll see worked examples of potentiodynamic polarisation, Tafel analysis, and linear polarisation resistance, plus practical checks you can apply to your own data before you trust it.

The recording includes the full live Q&A, so you can hear the audience's questions and the speaker's answers.

Key Takeaways
  • Choosing between Tafel, LPR and EIS
  • Cell and reference electrode setup
  • Scan rate and stabilisation time
  • Spotting bad Tafel fits
Best For
  • Corrosion researchers
  • Materials engineers
  • PhD students & postdocs
  • QA and testing labs
